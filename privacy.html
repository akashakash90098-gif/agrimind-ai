DATA INTEGRITY & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Transparent disclosure of data handling, browser storage, and client-side processing in AgriMind AI.

Effective Date: November 2026 • Platform Version: 1.0 (Static Frontend Prototype)

1. Client-Side Processing Architecture

AgriMind AI is engineered as a privacy-first static web application. All interactive features — including the AI Crop Doctor image scanning visualizer, Soil Intelligence pH gauge, Crop Planner suggestions, Mandi price sorting, and Farm Profit Calculator — execute entirely inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.

2. Handling of Uploaded Crop Photographs

When you select or drag-and-drop a crop leaf photograph in the AI Crop Doctor tool:

  • The image is loaded into your browser's local memory via the standard HTML5 FileReader API.
  • No image files are transmitted across the internet to any external server or third-party database.
  • No biometric or facial data is captured or retained.
  • When you refresh or close the browser tab, the previewed image is immediately purged from RAM.

3. Use of Browser LocalStorage

AgriMind AI uses your browser's local storage solely for preserving non-sensitive interface preferences across pages:

  • agrimind_lang: Stores your chosen language code (e.g. en, te, ta, or hi) so you do not have to reselect your preferred language upon clicking another tool.
  • We do not store passwords, tokens, API keys, location coordinates, or personal identifying data in localStorage.

4. Absence of User Accounts & Passwords

The current platform version does not include user registration, profiles, or authentication gates. All tools are open for exploration without creating an account or providing financial credentials.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Cookies

This prototype does not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies, behavioral remarketing pixels, or data-broker trackers. Fonts are delivered via Google Fonts with standard browser caching.

6. Future Live Integrations

If future versions integrate live e-NAM market feeds, server-hosted deep learning models, or satellite NDVI imagery, this policy will be formally updated to disclose any authenticated endpoints, server-side data logging, and encryption protocols.
