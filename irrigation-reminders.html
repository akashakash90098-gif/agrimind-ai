🍂 Signs of Water Stress (Under-Watering)

  • Mid-day leaf wilting and inward rolling
  • Premature flower drop or blossom end rot
  • Dull grayish-green leaf canopy

💧 Risks of Waterlogging (Over-Watering)

  • Lower leaf yellowing (root hypoxia & chlorosis)
  • Pythium root rot and damping-off disease
  • Nitrogen leaching beyond active root depth
